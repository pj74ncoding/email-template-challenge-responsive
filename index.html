Foxes Art Studios
All the artwork is created in our studios in the wonderful location here in Leicestershire. We cover many mediums including pastels, watercolour, acrylic, pen and ink and coloured pencils.


We also provide classes in all mediums with our experienced art tutors. There are weekend courses available throughout the year. Weather you are beginner or an experienced artist there is something for everyone.
